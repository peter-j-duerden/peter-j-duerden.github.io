Fretwork

A free guitar and bass tab editor for Windows. Write tab as fast as you can think it, print it cleanly, and hear it played back to check it's right.

Coming soon to the Microsoft Store · Windows 10 and 11

What it does

  • Fast keyboard entry. Type frets straight onto the strings, and pick note lengths, rests, ties, hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends, vibrato, harmonics and palm mutes with single keys.
  • Guitar and bass. 6-string guitar and 4- or 5-string bass, in standard and alternative tunings, with string names beside the tab.
  • Real notation. Rhythm under the tab, repeats with counts, first and second endings (even "1.–7."), one- and two-bar repeat signs, D.C. and D.S. al Coda, section labels, chord names and lyrics.
  • Print and PDF. A clean, compact page layout, with six lines of tab to a page.
  • Playback. Hear the tab with sampled guitar and bass sounds, following the repeats and endings. Loop a passage, count in, or slow it down to practise.
  • New from audio. Give it a recording, ideally one instrument on its own, such as a stem from Moises, and it writes a first draft of the tab. It works out the tempo and the chords, and writes repeated sections as repeats. It all happens on your PC.
  • Suggestions as you go. It notices repeated bars and offers to write them as repeats, and flags a bar that's too full or not full enough.
  • Your songs, your files. Songs are small files you keep wherever you like, with automatic recovery copies in case of a crash.

Fretwork is free, with no adverts. If it's helped you learn a song, a coffee is a lovely way to say thanks.
