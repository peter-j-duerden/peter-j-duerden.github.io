Fretwork Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Fretwork collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your songs or recordings anywhere.

Last updated 30 September 2026. This policy covers Fretwork. Resonance will be added here when it's released.

What stays on your PC

  • Your songs are saved as files where you choose, in Documents\Fretwork\Songs unless you pick somewhere else.
  • Settings, your chord library and recovery copies are kept in your Windows user profile. If you installed Fretwork from the Microsoft Store, Windows keeps them in the app's own storage. They're removed if you uninstall the app.
  • New from audio reads the recording you choose and works out the notes on your own PC, with a model built into the app. The recording is never uploaded.
  • Sound sets you add for playback stay in the folder you put them in.

The internet

Fretwork doesn't need the internet, and doesn't contact any server on its own. It only opens a web page when you click a link, such as the Buy me a coffee button in Settings. That page then opens in your browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

The Microsoft Store

If you install Fretwork from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and updates under its own privacy statement. Microsoft may give developers anonymous totals, such as the number of installs, and crash reports without personal details. I only use these to fix problems.

Children

Fretwork is suitable for all ages, and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, see the support page.
