Support

Found a problem, or have an idea? I'd love to hear it.

Report a problem or suggest a feature

This opens a form on GitHub, which needs a free GitHub account. Say which app and version you're using (in Fretwork, press F1: the version is next to the title), what you did, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

Where are my songs saved?

In Documents\Fretwork\Songs, unless you chose somewhere else. Settings → Open songs folder takes you there.

Fretwork closed unexpectedly. Is my work lost?

Probably not. While a song has unsaved changes, Fretwork keeps a recovery copy every couple of minutes, and offers it back the next time it opens.

How do I learn the keyboard shortcuts?

Press F1, or click ? in the title bar, for the list of shortcuts. Open the full guide there shows everything Fretwork does, with examples.

Why doesn't New from audio get every note right?

Working out notes from a recording is hard, even for people. It works best on one instrument on its own, such as a guitar or bass stem separated with Moises, and it gives you a strong first draft to correct. If the barlines land in the wrong place, press Alt + ← or Alt + → straight after importing to move them.

Can I make playback sound more realistic?

Yes. Fretwork can play with SoundFont (.sf2) files of real instruments. Settings → Playback sound explains how to add them.

Does Fretwork cost anything?

No. It's free, with no adverts. If you'd like to say thanks, you can buy me a coffee.
